Our process

We act for you, not the insurer.

01

Risk assessment

We identify your exposures and decide together what should be insured and what should be managed another way.

02

Market placement

We know the terms, exclusions and pricing across the market and place your cover where it actually fits.

03

Claims advocacy

We run the claim and deal with the insurer on your behalf. This is where a broker earns their fee.

04

Renewal review

Cover is re-tested against how your business has changed, not rolled over.

We save you time, money and worry. Buying on price alone backfires at claim stage, when you discover you were not adequately covered — or not covered at all.
